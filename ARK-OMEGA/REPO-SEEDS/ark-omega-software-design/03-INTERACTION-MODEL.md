## Selection semantics
Changing peer context changes the query/execution target, not merely presentation. AUTO delegates placement to HYPERNET policy. DIRECT binds to one eligible node. MESH fans approved read/sync operations to multiple authorized peers.

## Confirmation model
Read-only actions execute immediately. Mutating actions require authority evaluation. Destructive actions require explicit policy allowance and operator confirmation. High-impact commands expose target, effect, rollback, and proof expectation before dispatch.
