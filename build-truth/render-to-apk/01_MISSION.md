# 01 — Mission

Turn JANUS/ODIN Android phones into first-class embodied Ghost Atlas terminals without buying new hardware.

The phone must provide the practical functions expected from StackChan/AIBI/Alexa-class devices—voice, speaker, screen, camera, sensors, notifications, conversational presence, estate status, and governed commands—while using the existing Ghost Atlas cloud/software organism rather than duplicating it on-device.

Primary outcome: invoke Atlas from the phone, converse naturally, inspect live estate state, submit governed actions, and receive proof-backed completion.
