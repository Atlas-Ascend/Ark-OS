# 11 — VISHVARUPA / Hypernet Body Contract

JANUS and ODIN phones are VISHVARUPA body nodes, not merely clients.

Body attestation may report measured CPU, RAM, storage, network, battery, OS/app version, sensors, local runtimes and supported capabilities.

Example identity classes: `VISH-BODY-JANUS-001`, `VISH-BODY-ODIN-001`.

Eligibility requires fresh heartbeat/attestation. Declared capability is not runtime proof. Phone-local capabilities are routable through CrownGrid/Hypernet only when current policy and attestation permit them.
